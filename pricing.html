Pricing

Last updated 21 November 2026

Every galcode event has its own ticket price, shown on the event page and at checkout before you pay. Prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and the amount shown at checkout is the total you pay — there are no hidden charges.

How payment works

  • You pay online through our payment partner, Cashfree Payments, using UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking.
  • One ticket admits one person to one event.
  • What's included in the price (materials, food, entry) is listed on each event page.
  • Prices can change for future events, but never after you've paid for yours.
