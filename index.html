About galcode
Photo

galcode started with a simple idea: women grow faster when they have each other. We host small, thoughtful events across Chennai, from founder meetups to slow Sunday evenings, where you can walk in alone and leave with friends.

Founder's note
@ft.rakshaa

I wanted a space where women could show up exactly as they are, ambitious, curious, a little unsure, and find people who get it. That's what galcode is.

Raksha
