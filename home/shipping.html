Shipping & delivery policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

galcode sells tickets to in-person events. We don't sell or ship physical products.

How you receive your ticket

  • Your ticket appears on screen as soon as your payment succeeds, with a unique ticket number.
  • We also confirm your booking on the phone number or email you gave us, usually within 24 hours.
  • Show your ticket number or the phone number you booked with at the venue.

Service delivery

The service is delivered at the event itself, on the date and at the venue shown on the event page and your ticket.

Didn't get your ticket?

If your payment went through but you don't see a ticket, email thisisgalcode@gmail.com or call +91 80150 64473 and we'll sort it out.
