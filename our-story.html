01 · The beginning

Why I started galcode

I wanted a space where women could show up exactly as they are: ambitious, curious, a little unsure. A place to find people who get it. I couldn't find one in Chennai, so I started building it.

galcode started with a simple idea: women grow faster when they have each other. No pressure to already know anyone, no need to have it all figured out. Just show up.

“Walk in alone, leave with friends.”The galcode promise
02 · What we do

Small rooms, real friends

We host small, thoughtful events across Chennai: workshops, founder meetups, creative sessions, outings and slow Sunday evenings. Every one of them is girls-only, and every one is built so the quiet girl who came alone has someone to talk to within ten minutes.

It's less networking, more making things together. You leave with something you made, a few new numbers in your phone, and usually a plan for next week.

03 · The vision

Where we're going

This is just the first chapter. I want galcode to be the first place a girl thinks of when she wants to try something new, meet her people, or start the thing she's been scared to start.

More events, more cities, and more girls finding their people. One small room at a time.
