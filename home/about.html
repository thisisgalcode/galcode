About us

Last updated 22 November 2026

galcode began with a simple idea: a space in Chennai made by a girl, for girls — where showing up alone never feels lonely, and every room is full of women you'd want to know.

What started in May 2026 has since grown into more than ten meetups and ten events, each one bringing new faces into the fold. And we're only getting started — bigger gatherings are already in the works.

Who runs galcode

galcode is a proprietary business run by Raksha, registered as a micro enterprise under the Government of India's Udyam (MSME) scheme (registration UDYAM-TN-34-0111682).

What we sell

We sell tickets to galcode events. Each event page lists the date, time, venue, what's included and the ticket price. When you buy a ticket, you get a confirmed spot at that event.

Who can join

Our events are for women only. Some events have an age limit, which is shown on the event page.
