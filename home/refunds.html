Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

Here's how cancellations and refunds work for galcode event tickets.

If you cancel

There's no refund if you cancel. Tickets are transferable, not refundable — if you can't attend, you can pass your ticket to someone else who meets the event's rules. Just send us their name and phone number before the event. No-shows aren't refunded.

If galcode cancels the event

If we cancel an event, you get a full refund of the amount you paid.

How refunds are paid

When an event is cancelled by us, we'll contact everyone who booked. Refunds go back to the same bank account or payment method you paid with, within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

Questions

Email thisisgalcode@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 80150 64473 with your name, phone number and ticket number.
