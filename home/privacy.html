Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information galcode collects, why, and how we keep it safe.

What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, phone number, email address and age, and which event you booked.
  • Payment status: whether your payment succeeded and its reference number. Your card, UPI or bank details are handled only by our payment partner, Cashfree Payments — we never receive or store them.
  • Site usage: simple, anonymous visit counts so we can see which events people look at.

Why we use it

  • To confirm your booking, send you your ticket and event updates, and let you in at the venue.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To plan better events.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the services that help us run galcode: Cashfree Payments (payments), Supabase (secure database) and Vercel (website hosting), and only as much as they need to do their job. We may also share it if the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them for the event and for our accounts, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing thisisgalcode@gmail.com.
