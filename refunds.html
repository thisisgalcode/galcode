Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

We know plans change. Here's how cancellations and refunds work for galcode event tickets.

If you cancel

  • 48 hours or more before the event starts: full refund.
  • Less than 48 hours before the event: no refund, but you can transfer your ticket to another woman who meets the event's rules — just tell us her name and number before the event.
  • No-shows: no refund.

If galcode cancels or changes the event

  • If we cancel an event, you get a full refund.
  • If we change the date or venue and you can't make it, you can ask for a full refund.

How to ask for a refund

Email thisisgalcode@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 80150 64473 with your name, phone number and ticket number.

When you'll get your money

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.
